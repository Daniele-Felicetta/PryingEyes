# Glossary

The words of the game and of the project, in alphabetical order inside each group. The Italian name is the one the game shows, where it has one. Each entry links to the page that explains it.

## Modes and time

- **Candle cycle** (`-candles`): the legacy cycle of the standard mode, with stealth and candle rounds. See [the candle cycle](cycles/candle-cycle.md).
- **Day and night**: in the desert a day lasts twenty minutes, about twelve of daylight and eight of night. See [day and night](cycles/day-night.md).
- **Desert**: the open-world mode of sand, built from one seed, with no waves. See [the desert](modes/desert.md).
- **Procession of lights** (Processione dei Lumi): every fifth wave of the wave cycle, made only of Veglie. See [the Veglia](enemies/veglia.md).
- **Sandstorm**: the desert's weather, about one day in two, between 08:00 and 22:00. See [the sandstorm](cycles/sandstorm.md).
- **Standard mode**: the game of the cathedral, the catacombs, the waves and the trials. See [standard mode](modes/standard.md).
- **Stealth**: the rules by which enemies notice you, active only in the candle cycle. See [stealth](systems/stealth.md).
- **Trial** (prova): an optional goal on an even wave of the wave cycle: Testa, Coltello, Esplosivi, Intatto or [Furia](blessings/furia.md). See [Trial: Testa](trials/testa.md).
- **Wave cycle**: the default cycle of the standard mode, with waves of enemies. See [the wave cycle](cycles/wave-cycle.md).

## Places and structures

- **Abyss** (Abisso): the fifth gate of the standard catacombs, opened by the Rosone. See [the Abyss](zones/abyss.md).
- **Altar of Consecration** (altare della consacrazione): the desert's altar, which consecrates the weapon in your hand. See [the Altar of Consecration](works/altare-della-consacrazione.md).
- **Annex**: a hidden room of a tower floor in the wave cycle, behind its own gate. See [annexes](systems/annexes.md).
- **Armoury** (armeria): the desert's shop in the cathedral, which sells every weapon at twice the standard price. See [the cathedral](zones/cathedral.md#standard-and-desert).
- **Branch** (ramo): one of the four branches of the standard catacombs, three levels each with its boss. See [L'Ossario](branches/ossario.md).
- **Camp**: a rest place of the desert, with a campfire that burns at night. See [the campfire](camps/campfire.md).
- **Catacombs** (catacombe): the standard way down, under the cathedral. See [the catacombs](zones/catacombs.md).
- **Cathedral** (cattedrale): the base, with the square, the market, the works and the five gates behind the apse. See [the cathedral](zones/cathedral.md).
- **Catacomb stack**: the desert's catacomb, three to five levels built on the sand. See [the desert catacomb](stacks/catacomb.md).
- **Gate** (cancello): a stone landing that closes a branch or a stair until it is opened. See [the portcullis](gates/portcullis.md).
- **Landmark**: a tall thing on the sand that shows from afar and helps you find your way. See [the obelisk](landmarks/obelisk.md).
- **Level** (livello): one floor of a branch: the first level, the middle floors and the boss level. See [the first level](levels/first-level.md).
- **Library of Vows** (biblioteca dei voti): the desert's library in the cathedral, which sells perk books for points. See [the Library of Vows](works/biblioteca-dei-voti.md).
- **Paid door**: an area door that you open with points, for everyone, for the rest of the floor. See [the area door](doors/area-door.md).
- **Place**: a building on the sand, such as a house, an inn, a chapel, a church, a hotel, a theatre, a sanatorium or a complex. See [the hotel](places/hotel.md).
- **Region**: one of the six areas of the desert, each with its own ground and places. See [the dunes](regions/dunes.md).
- **Shutter** (serranda): the steel shutter that closes the lifts of the wave floors until three waves are cleared. See [the shutter](gates/shutter.md).
- **Stack**: a desert tower or catacomb, a stack of floors on the sand with its boss in the hall. See [the tower](stacks/tower.md).
- **Stall** (banco): a stall of the cathedral's market. See [the apothecary](stalls/speziale.md).
- **Tower** (torre): the desert's stack of seven to fourteen floors, with its boss on the top floor. See [the tower](stacks/tower.md).
- **Work** (opera): a building of the cathedral that costs resources and gives an effect. See [the forge](works/la-forgia.md).

## People, enemies and animals

- **Abbot** (abate): a walker, and the guardian of the exit lift on the standard floors. See [the abbot](enemies/abbot.md).
- **Boss**: the enemy at the end of a branch or a stack. The four branch bosses are the Cardinale, the Direttore, the Primario and the Conte. See [the Cardinale](enemies/cardinale.md).
- **Cardinale**: the boss of the Ossario, branch I, and the boss that opens every eighth wave. See [the Cardinale](enemies/cardinale.md).
- **Conte**: the boss of the Guardaroba del Conte, branch IV. See [the Conte](enemies/conte.md).
- **Creature**: an animal of the desert: a lizard, a scarab, a scorpion or a vulture. See [the lizard](creatures/lizard.md).
- **Direttore**: the boss of the Teatro sepolto, branch II. See [the Direttore](enemies/direttore.md).
- **Flying eye** (occhio volante): an eye that flies instead of walking; in the desert it flies only at night. See [the flying eye](enemies/flying-eye.md).
- **Infermiere** (the nurse): the walker of the sanatorium. See [the Infermiere](enemies/infermiere.md).
- **Lord of the place**: the desert boss of a tower or a catacomb, one of the four branch bosses, chosen by the place. See [the lord of the place](enemies/signore-del-luogo.md).
- **Maschera** (the mask): the walker of the theatres. See [the Maschera](enemies/maschera.md).
- **Monk** (monaco): the stealth enemy of the floors; other enemies are built on its body. See [the monk](enemies/monk.md).
- **Primario**: the boss of the Sanatorio, branch III, who calls two nurses. See [the Primario](enemies/primario.md).
- **Survivor** (sopravvissuto): one of the four playable survivors. See [the Custode](survivors/custode.md).
- **Veglia** (plural Veglie): the enemy that carries a candle, and the enemy of the procession. See [the Veglia](enemies/veglia.md).
- **Walker**: an enemy that moves on foot in the open or on the floors, as against a boss or a flying eye. See [the monk](enemies/monk.md).

## Objects and resources

- **Blessing** (benedizione): a boon chosen at the altar before a catacomb descent; standard only. See [the blessings](systems/blessings.md).
- **Darkness crystal** (cristallo di oscurità): the Direttore's resource; the Sanatorio gate costs one. See [darkness crystals](resources/cristalli-d-oscurita.md).
- **Faith crystal** (cristallo di fede): the Primario's resource; the Conte's gate costs one. See [faith crystals](resources/cristalli-di-fede.md).
- **Gems** (gemme): the Conte's resource, which builds the Vetrata del Conte. See [gems](resources/gemme.md).
- **Grade** (grado): one of the five grades of weapon, from I to V. See [grade I](grades/grade-1.md).
- **Grimoire** (grimorio): a magic book of the standard floors: the Book of the Arsenal or the Book of Witchcraft. See [the Book of the Arsenal](grimoires/arsenal.md).
- **Hazard**: a fixture of the floors that falls or burns, such as a barrel, a chandelier or a lamp. See [chandeliers](hazards/chandeliers.md).
- **Motorbike** (moto): the desert's vehicle, one for each player. See [the motorbike](vehicles/moto.md).
- **Oboli** (oboli): the coin of the market, which a catacomb descent brings back, one for every ten points. See [points and oboli](systems/points-and-oboli.md).
- **Perk**: one of eight effects from the magic books of the libraries; you hold up to four. See [perks](systems/perks.md).
- **Pickup**: an object on the sand or the floors that you walk over to take, such as a medikit. See [the medikit](pickups/medikit.md).
- **Points** (punti): the score that buys doors, perks, weapons and power-ups. See [points and oboli](systems/points-and-oboli.md).
- **Power-up**: an object that gives an effect for a while, such as the Reliquiario or the [Campana](power-ups/campana.md). See [the Reliquiario](power-ups/reliquiario.md).
- **Prop** (arredo): a piece of furniture or a fitting that dresses a room. See [the dining table](props/arr-tavolo.md).
- **Relic** (reliquia): the Ossario's rare resource, the Cardinale's trophy. The Teatro gate costs one. See [relics](resources/reliquie.md).
- **Resource** (risorsa): steel, wood, bones, blood, hearts, relics and the rare crystals, spent on the works. See [steel](resources/acciaio.md).
- **Satchel**: the expedition's bag, banked in the cathedral's store on return; standard only. See [the satchel](systems/satchel.md).
- **Seal** (sigillo): a node of the Book of the Arsenal; some are traced on the weapon in your hand. See [the seal Incendiario](seals/incendiario.md).
- **Spell**: an inner node of the Book of Witchcraft, bought in the book's secret room. See [the Book of Witchcraft](grimoires/witchcraft.md).
- **Throwable**: an item thrown from the hand, such as holy water or the carillon. See [holy water](throwables/holy-water.md).
- **Trap**: a device that works against the enemies, such as the reliquary mine. See [the reliquary mine](traps/reliquary-mine.md).
- **Weapon** (arma): one of the 28 weapons, from the Pietà P-9, the starting pistol, to the Altare 50 of grade V. See [the Pietà P-9](weapons/pieta-p9.md).

## Systems and controls

- **Co-op** (cooperativa): up to four players share a tower or the desert over the internet. See [co-op](systems/co-op.md).
- **Characters and saves**: a character is a name with the look of one of the four survivors, and it keeps its cathedral and its history. See [characters and saves](systems/saves-and-characters.md).
- **Controller**: a gamepad; the game adopts the first pad that presses a button. See [controller](systems/controller.md).
- **Downed**: at zero health you crawl instead of dying, until a teammate revives you. See [downed and revive](systems/downed-and-revive.md).
- **Floor power** (quadro elettrico): the electrical panel of the wave floors; pulling its lever brings the power back. See [floor power](systems/floor-power.md).
- **Knife** (coltello): the close-range weapon, on G. In the candle cycle a takedown from behind is silent. See [the knife](systems/knife.md).
- **Medal**: an instant feat, such as a chain of kills, counted on the scoreboard. See [medals](systems/medals.md).
- **Minimap**: the desert's chart of the sand, north up, with the golden arrow home. See [minimap and way home](systems/minimap-and-way-home.md).
- **Seat** (posto): one player's place in a split screen. See [split screen](systems/split-screen.md).
- **Split screen** (schermo condiviso): two to four people on one PC, each with a seat. See [split screen](systems/split-screen.md).
- **Third person** (terza persona): the camera behind the survivor's shoulder, under the Prospettiva setting. See [third person](systems/third-person.md).

## The project

- **Blender models**: the scripts that make the weapons and the figures, in `modelli/`. See [Blender models](pipeline/blender-models.md).
- **Check suite**: `tools/check_suite.py`, the boundary and documentation checks run before a commit. See [check suite](pipeline/check-suite.md).
- **CI gate**: the checks that run on every push to preview and on every pull request. See [CI gate](pipeline/ci-gate.md).
- **Concept map**: the diagram of the main concepts, drawn from `wiki/concepts.json`. See [concept map](concept-map.md).
- **Lab**: an engine-free test program for a package, run without the editor. See [labs](pipeline/labs.md).
- **Package**: a production package under `unity/PryingEyes/Packages`, with no engine references. See [the saves package](packages/saves.md).
- **Release**: a zip of a version, prepared by `tools/release.ps1`, which publishes nothing. See [release](pipeline/release.md).
- **Ship**: `tools/ship.ps1`, which commits, builds, smoke-tests and pushes the work, and refreshes the game. See [ship](pipeline/ship.md).
- **Studio engine**: the production engine that runs jobs with reports, in standard-library Python. See [studio engine](pipeline/studio-engine.md).
- **Wiki generator**: `tools/wiki_site.py`, which checks the pages and builds the catalogue and the site. See [wiki generator](pipeline/wiki-generator.md).
