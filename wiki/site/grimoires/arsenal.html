The Book of the Arsenal

The Book of the Arsenal is the book of the weapons. Its outer nodes work on every weapon you hold, and its inner nodes are the seals that you trace on the weapon with the left hand. It has the same 19-node shape as the Book of Witchcraft.

How it is bought

  • Outer nodes. They can always be bought. Each costs 1,250 points, plus 250 for each outer node you already own in this book.
  • Inner nodes. Each needs one of its two neighbouring outer nodes. It costs 3,000 points, plus 500 for each inner node you own.
  • Supreme. It needs four inner nodes, and it costs 9,000 points.
  • Keeping. Purchases last the whole run. Dying empties the essence and turns off the seals on the weapons.

The seals

The six inner nodes are the seals: Danni Critici, Sanguinamento, Incendiario, Corrosivo, Elettrificato and Gelido. Each seal lasts 45 seconds for 40 essence. The supreme node is Arma Maledetta.

Standard and desert

  • Standard. Every catacomb floor has its secret book room, which holds this book or the Book of Witchcraft.
  • Desert. The desert doc describes no secret book room and no grimoire; see the Book of Witchcraft.
