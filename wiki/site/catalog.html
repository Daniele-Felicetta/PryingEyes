Modes

PageModeSummary
Standard modeStandardThe game of the cathedral: a base you rebuild, the catacombs under it with four branches and their bosses, and the waves and trials that come at the top.
The desertDesertThe open world of sand: one seed, six regions, towers and catacombs on the sand, a day of twenty minutes, sandstorms, a motorbike and four bosses; no waves, no stealth, nothing saved.

Regions

PageModeSummary

The dunes (region)DesertThe dunes are the desert region of the highest transverse dunes: crests across the wind, 120 to 230 m apart, 9 m high near the cathedral and 18 m far out.
The hamlets (region)DesertThe hamlets are the desert region of dry earth with many small houses, inns, wells and windmills; the cathedral always stands in it, and it has the most places of any region.
The holy land (region)DesertThe holy land is the desert region of pale chalk with chapels, churches, columns, steles and obelisks, and it has catacombs among its places.
The salt flats (region)DesertThe salt flats are the desert region of white, flat and almost empty ground, with bones and white rocks, a film of water over a third of its interior, and almost no dunes.
