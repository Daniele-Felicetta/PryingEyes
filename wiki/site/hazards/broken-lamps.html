Standard

Broken lamps

Every electric lamp that is a visible fixture of a standard floor can be broken with one shot. During a fight the lamps go out one after another, and the room really gets darker.

What it is

  • Kinds. Six names are on the list: Abatjour (table lamp), Lampadina (hanging bulb), Applique (electric wall lamp), arr_lampada (floor lamp, see the floor lamp), Neon (neon tube) and AstaScialitica (surgical lamp).
  • Not breakable. The candle wall lamps are not electric and do not break.
  • Target. A small sphere collider on each lamp is the bullet target.

How it works

  • After the floor is built, the code finds the pool lights flagged as electric whose parent has one of those names.
  • Breaking a lamp takes its light out of the pool, stops its flicker (LampFlicker, NeonFlicker), swaps its bulb, shade and tube to unlit materials, plays a procedural pop with glass shards (SoundGen.BulbPop) and throws sparks.
  • In co-op a guest sends LampHit, the host breaks the lamp and sends LampBroken to everyone, and late joiners receive the broken ones without effects (protocol pe-coop-25).

Open question: the floor lamp's shade

The breakable-lamps note says that a shot floor lamp's shade and bulb swap to their unlit materials (paralume_spento, lampadina_spenta). The phase-5 note of 17 November 2026 found the opposite is probable: the swap happens only when a material's name contains "paralume", "lampadina" or "neon", and the floor lamp's materials are named A_paralume and A_lampadina, which map to the existing shade and bulb assets. So the shade probably is not swapped, and a shot floor lamp probably keeps glowing. Both notes were read in the code, and neither was tried in the game.

Verification

PryingEyes.exe -batchmode -autotest -nofire -noenemies -lamptest [-lampshots] -seed 1000 -floor 1 runs ten checks on the lamps: they exist, none is a candle, a lit one is hit by a real bullet, its light leaves the pool, the target disappears, the materials turn unlit and a second shot does nothing. The church floor of seed 1000 has five electric wall lamps; the hotel floors have more.

Standard and desert

The lamps break on the standard floors, once they are built. No desert script calls the breaking code, so the desert pages do not document shot lamps. The desert's lamps come from the same room kits, and whether they break there is not known.
