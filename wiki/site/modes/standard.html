Standard

Standard mode

Standard mode is the game the project was built around. You start in a cathedral that you rebuild with the points and the resources you bring back from the catacombs. Under it, the catacombs are floors in four branches, each with its own boss behind a gate. The enemies come at you in waves (the default) or, in the legacy candle cycle, you sneak past them.

The cathedral

The cathedral is the base: the square and its market outside, the works inside, and the five gates behind the apse that lead down. The works cost steel, wood, bones or the rare resources. The forge adds 15% damage with every weapon, and the organ heals you fully after every wave you clear. The market trades with you: the apothecary sells holy water and candles, the bone cart sells lots of bones, and the surgeon's tent sells one rare resource at a time.

The catacombs

The catacombs are the way down. Each branch is three levels: the first level, the middle floors, and the boss level, where the branch's boss waits behind its gate. The branches are:

  • L'Ossario, the first, open from the start, with the Cardinale as its boss;
  • Il Teatro sepolto, the second, with the Direttore;
  • Il Sanatorio, the third, with the Primario;
  • Il Guardaroba del Conte, the fourth, with the Conte.

Beyond them is the Abyss, the fifth gate, opened by the Rosone. The gates close the stone landings: a portcullis rises when the way is open, and the shutters close the lifts of the wave floors until three waves are cleared.

The floors are full of monks, flagellanti, statues, appestati and veglie. The medusa flies over the lower floors. The stealth rules decide how they notice you, but only in the candle cycle.

Waves, trials and the procession

The wave cycle starts each wave on its own: 16 enemies plus 4 per wave, a procession of lights every fifth wave and a Cardinale every eighth. From the second wave, every even wave that is not a procession offers a trial: Testa, Coltello, Esplosivi, Intatto or Furia.

Points, perks and magic

Points buy doors, perks, weapons and power-ups (see points and oboli). The points you bring back from a catacomb expedition become oboli, one for every ten points. The perks are eight magic books in the libraries. The two grimoires, The Book of Witchcraft and The Book of the Arsenal, hold the spells and the seals. The altar offers a blessing before each descent.

Standard and desert

  • Standard. This mode: the cathedral, the catacombs, the waves and the trials. Its candle cycle is still there behind the -candles flag.
  • Desert. The other mode is one open world of sand, with no waves, no catacombs and no stealth. Its towers and catacombs are stacks built on the sand. See the desert and standard vs desert.
