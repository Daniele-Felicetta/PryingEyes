Standard

The catacombs

The catacombs are the procedural floors of the standard game. They used to be reached by a lift in a tower; since 17 November 2026 they are reached by going down, through the gates behind the cathedral's apse. Every floor is generated from the seed and is the same every time you come back to it.

How a descent goes

  1. You enter through an open gate, on the first level of a branch.
  2. Each level plays like a floor of the old Tower: waves, paid doors, perks, the mystery chest, the altar, the traps and the hazards.
  3. Enemies drop resources on the floor. Walking close pulls them into your satchel (zaino).
  4. You come back up in one of three ways: by the stair you came down, between waves; by the gate after the boss of the third level, which leads up; or with the return bell, once per expedition.
  5. Coming back alive, the unspent points become oboli. Dying ends the expedition: you wake in the cathedral with half of the common resources, and the rest is left on the first level as your remains.

The branches

BranchThemeBoss on the third level
L'Ossariohotel and churchIl Cardinale
Il Teatro sepoltotheatreIl Direttore
Il SanatoriosanatoriumIl Primario
Il Guardaroba del ContewardrobeIl Conte

After the fourth branch comes the Abyss. The difficulty grows with the floor number, which is the branch number times three plus the level: the first branch is floors 1 to 3, the second floors 4 to 6, and so on.

Standard and desert

The catacombs exist only in the standard game. In the desert the same idea is a stack: a tower of three to five levels with its boss in the hall of the last level, entered by a stair from the sand. There are no gates, no branches and no satchel in the desert. See the desert.
