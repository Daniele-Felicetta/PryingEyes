Standard vs desert

The two modes share the cathedral, its market and its works, the weapons, the saves and the menus. They differ in the time, the enemies, the way down and the money. The table below is the short version. Each page has its own Standard and desert section, which says what changes and what does not; the page's Sources line says where that comes from.

Each row of the lists gives one difference in a line. Mode says where the page belongs: Standard, Desert, or Both when the same thing exists in both. The pipeline, package and project pages have no mode: they describe the tools and the history, not the game.

At a glance

TopicStandardDesert
TimeThe wave cycle (the default), or the candle cycle with -candlesOne day of twenty minutes, with night and sandstorms
StealthOnly in the candle cycleNone: the enemies rest until you come near, then they hunt
TrialsOffered on the even waves, except processionsNone
The way downThe five gates behind the apse lead to the catacombsTowers and catacombs are stacks built on the sand; the gates are not built
BossesThe four branch bosses behind their gates; in the Abyss they come in turnThe same four, placed by the lord of the place at the top or bottom of each stack
Points and oboliPoints from kills; oboli from a catacomb descent, one for every ten pointsPoints are gold coins on the sand; nothing is brought back to turn into oboli
WeaponsFound on the floors, and drawn by the ScrignoGrade I free on the sand, grade II in a boss hall; the armoury sells the rest at twice the price
PerksEight, in the libraries of the floors; the wave cycle leaves out twoEight, in the Library of Vows, at the same prices
BlessingsChosen at the altar before each descentNone
VehiclesNoneOne motorbike for each player
SavesThe cathedral and the expeditionNothing of the desert; the cathedral, its store and its works are kept
Co-op and split screenOne game shared by allOne world per machine, built from the host's seed; each seat has its own expedition

Standard modeStandardThe cathedral, the catacombs, the waves and the trials; the candle cycle stays behind -candles.
The desertDesertOne open world of sand: no waves, no candles, no stealth, no catacomb expedition, and nothing saved.
