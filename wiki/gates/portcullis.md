# Portcullis (the iron gate)

![An exit with its portcullis raised](../img/gates/portcullis.webp)

Since 17 November 2026 the lifts of the Tower are the gates of the catacombs: a stone landing closed by an iron portcullis that rises
(`ProcLift.BuildGate`), instead of a steel cabin.

## How it works

- **The arrival gate** has the steps you came down on one side, and the portcullis you came through.
- **The exit gate** has the dark shaft where the stair goes on, a torch on the wall and an iron lantern over the arch.
- **The lantern lights** when the portcullis opens, so you can see that the way is open from a distance.

Three works of the cathedral, *Il Cancello del Teatro*, *del Sanatorio* and *del Conte*, decide which branches are open; the first branch
is open from the start. See [the Cancello del Teatro](../works/il-cancello-del-teatro.md).

## Standard and desert

Only the standard game has these gates. The desert does not build the descent gates: its towers and catacombs are stacks with stairs.
See [the desert](../modes/desert.md) and [the stacks](../stacks/catacomb.md).
