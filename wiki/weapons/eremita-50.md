# EREMITA .50

![The Eremita .50 held in the hand, from the game](../img/weapons/eremita-50.webp)

The Eremita is a magnum pistol: one heavy shot that goes through two enemies, with a small magazine.

## Numbers

| Grade | Class | Damage | Fire | Magazine | Sight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [IV](../grades/grade-4.md) | magnum pistol | 125, pierces 2 | semi | 7 | adjustable iron sights |

## Standard and desert

The same weapon in both modes. In the desert it is in the armoury at twice the standard price. See [the desert](../modes/desert.md).
