# Standard vs desert

The two modes share the cathedral, its market and its works, the weapons, the saves and the menus. They differ in the time, the enemies, the way down and the money. The table below is the short version. Each page has its own Standard and desert section, which says what changes and what does not; the page's Sources line says where that comes from.

Each row of the lists gives one difference in a line. Mode says where the page belongs: Standard, Desert, or Both when the same thing exists in both. The pipeline, package and project pages have no mode: they describe the tools and the history, not the game.

## At a glance

| Topic | Standard | Desert |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time | The wave cycle (the default), or the candle cycle with `-candles` | One day of twenty minutes, with night and sandstorms |
| Stealth | Only in the candle cycle | None: the enemies rest until you come near, then they hunt |
| Trials | Offered on the even waves, except processions | None |
| The way down | The five gates behind the apse lead to the catacombs | Towers and catacombs are stacks built on the sand; the gates are not built |
| Bosses | The four branch bosses behind their gates; in the Abyss they come in turn | The same four, placed by the lord of the place at the top or bottom of each stack |
| Points and oboli | Points from kills; oboli from a catacomb descent, one for every ten points | Points are gold coins on the sand; nothing is brought back to turn into oboli |
| Weapons | Found on the floors, and drawn by the Scrigno | Grade I free on the sand, grade II in a boss hall; the armoury sells the rest at twice the price |
| Perks | Eight, in the libraries of the floors; the wave cycle leaves out two | Eight, in the Library of Vows, at the same prices |
| Blessings | Chosen at the altar before each descent | None |
| Vehicles | None | One motorbike for each player |
| Saves | The cathedral and the expedition | Nothing of the desert; the cathedral, its store and its works are kept |
| Co-op and split screen | One game shared by all | One world per machine, built from the host's seed; each seat has its own expedition |
