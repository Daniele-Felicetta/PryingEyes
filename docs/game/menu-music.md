# Menu music and interface tones

The main menu has a theme of its own (0.8.0): a waltz for music box and organ, generated by the game at startup (no audio file), and four
short tones for buttons, choices and sliders. The pause screen has no theme: there the music of the wave decides
([feel-and-reflections.md](feel-and-reflections.md)). The settings that control it are in [display-and-menu.md](display-and-menu.md).

## The theme

D minor, 3/4, 60 beats a minute, 32 bars: 96 seconds, stereo, 22.05 kHz, written so that the end falls back on the start without a click.
Code: `SoundMenu.cs` (`SoundGen.MenuTheme` and the score), `MenuMusic.cs` (playback and the clip).

| Part | Bars | Chords (one a bar) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A, the theme (dry) | 1 to 8 | Dm Dm Bb A7, Dm Gm A7 Dm |
| B, the contrast in F major, with a crystal echo an octave up | 9 to 16 | F F C C, Bb Gm A7 A7 |
| A', the theme going out of tune (some notes detuned at random, an E-flat against the D) | 17 to 24 | as A |
| C, the coda, ending on C sharp: the leading tone that leads back to the D of the next lap | 25 to 32 | Dm Bb Gm A7, Dm Gm A7 A7 |

Voices: the music box (a steel comb: the note and a slightly detuned copy that beats softly, plus the cantilever-beam modes at 6.27 and
17.55 times the note, which make the "tin" of the attack and die at once; higher notes are shorter), the organ holding each chord (a
band-limited saw with a detuned copy on the left and one on the right, a slow vibrato, a one-second attack), a pedal note that breathes through
the bar, a low distant bell in D (bronze partials, the minor third on top) and a stone hall around everything (Schroeder combs and
all-passes, treated as periodic so the tail of the end falls on the start, with different lengths on the two channels). The notes are
two-pole resonators, not a sine per sample, so the whole theme is computed in a thread when the menu is created
(`SoundBank.WarmMenuMusic`); `MenuMusicReview` fails above 10 seconds.

## When it plays

`MenuMusic` fades the theme in over 2.5 seconds as soon as the menu is open and the clip is ready, keeps it going through the screens that
open from the menu (settings, guide, co-op, split screen), fades it out over 1.5 seconds when the game starts and begins it again from the
start when you return to the menu. Its volume is the clip times `MenuMusic.Level` (0.16), **Tema del menu** and **Musica**, and the master
volume through the listener. It does not go through the room echo. While it plays, `ProcAmbience` lowers its own organ and tones by up to 60%
(`MenuMusic.Duck`); the duck is zero when either slider is under 2%. `-nomusic` and every automated run (`GameSettings.QuietRun`: any
`-*test` argument) leave it out, so an agent testing the game does not play music on the maintainer's computer.

## Loudness

The clip is a full-scale master: peak 0.85 (-1.4 dBFS), RMS -10.1 dBFS, crest 8.7 dB, **-7.8 LUFS** over the 96 seconds (3-second windows from
-10.4 to -4.2 LUFS, median -7.7; parts A, B, A' and C -7.5, -7.7, -7.1 and -9.3). `MenuMusic.Level` brings it to about **-23.7 LUFS** in the
game: between the ambience bed (-28.8) and the music of the waves (-25 to -22 with its own bus of 0.65), the figures noted when the level
was chosen. The two ends of the range can be moved by the player with the sliders.

## What was checked, and what was not

`PryingEyes.EditorTools.MenuMusicReview.Run` (an editor method, run in batch mode; it exits by itself) computes the theme and the four
tones, checks them and writes `menu_theme.wav`, `ui_tick.wav`, `ui_step.wav`, `ui_confirm.wav`, `ui_back.wav` and `result.txt` in
`unity_logs/menu-music` (not in git). On 17 November 2026 it passed 24 of 24 checks:

- the theme: 96 s of stereo, every sample finite, peak 0.85, clip RMS and playback RMS in range (-14 to -6 and -32 to -20 dBFS), left and
  right within 3 dB, correlation between 0.2 and 0.97 (not dual mono), no second quieter than -50 dBFS, the jump at the loop seam no larger
  than three times the 99th-percentile step on both channels, the first and last quarter second within 10 dB, and the clip the game builds
  (stereo, 22.05 kHz, 96 s) after its thread;
- the score: every bar of three beats, at least 60 notes, all between D4 and E6, at most four strong-beat notes outside their chord, the last
  note the C sharp of bar 32, every chord known;
- the maths of `MenuMusic.TargetVolume`;
- each tone: finite, peak 0.3 to 0.7, 0.05 to 1 s (measured: tick 90 ms, peak -6.0 dBFS; step 80 ms, -6.9; confirm 700 ms, -4.4;
  back 500 ms, -5.2).

The rest was measured on the WAV outside the editor with a short Python script (numpy, scipy, pyloudnorm; the script is not in the
repository): the loudness above; 32 of 32 bars with more than half of their pitch-class energy in the notes of their chord; an obvious attack
(band above 3 kHz) on 95 of the 96 beats, 7.2 times the level between beats; the bass 1.17 times stronger at the start of a bar than in the
middle; the loop seam without a click (the high-frequency peak within 0.3 s of the joint is 5.9 times the median, a click would give more
than 20, and the jump is 0.0101 against a typical step of 0.148); left and right correlation 0.80 (60 to 300 Hz), 0.42 (300 to 1200 Hz) and
0.34 (1200 to 4000 Hz), side energy 4.9 dB under the middle; spectral centroid 612 Hz (443 to 941 Hz for 80% of the time), 0.0003% of the
energy above 8 kHz.

**Not verified**: nobody has listened to it. The numbers say that nothing is broken, in tune with its chords, on the beat, steady in level
and seamless; they do not say that it sounds good, that the tones are pleasant, or that the level suits a living room. That is the
maintainer's ear. The tones and the theme were not played in a real player window either.
